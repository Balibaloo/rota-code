Only the principal can say which stands. `msg.clarify_principal` with refs to
both, and present them as they are: the two statements, side by side, in their
own words.

Do not resolve it, do not rank them by recency, and do not say which you think
they meant. You have the one piece of information that makes this decidable —
what they actually wrote — and adding an opinion to it makes it harder to answer,
not easier.
