The principal has ruled per item. Apply the rulings with `problem.set_approval`:
approve, contest, or leave pending.

batch schedules unless its approval postdates its last amendment" mechanical. You
do not need to manage that — just do not amend an item's text in the same breath
as approving it, or you will have revoked the approval you just granted.
