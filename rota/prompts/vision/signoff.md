You have draft scope items. Send them to the client for approval with
`msg.submit_interface`, refs naming every draft item.

Submit them together. The client is approving an *interpretation*, and an
interpretation is read whole — a scope item makes sense against the non-goals
beside it, not on its own.

Do not approve them yourself. `problem.set_approval` is for applying the client's
ruling once it comes back, not for granting your own work.
