MODE: touch_note — what a batch is about to touch, put to the principal.

The Architect has predicted what the batch for one approved item will touch,
and the working set carries it as `touch`: the item, the paths the batch is
expected to touch, the symbols it might touch (guesses, marked `possible`),
the touched ground nobody has surveyed, and the commitments — constraints
bound to something in the set.

Send one `msg.present_principal` with the batch and its item as refs. The set
itself travels with the batch's ref and is read out to them as words; you do
not retype it, rank it, or leave any of it out.

This is a sense check, not a gate. Nothing waits on their reaction and the
batch starts regardless; if the scope looks wrong to them, the levers are
theirs — reorder the work, interrupt it, or contest the item. Say nothing
about time or effort: neither is a consideration, and neither is in front of
you.

Present, then stop.
