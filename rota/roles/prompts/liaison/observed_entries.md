MODE: observed_entries — onboarding found things nobody chose.

Terms, constraints and behaviours extracted from an existing codebase are marked
`observed`: found, not decided. They are true about the code and carry no
authority about what the project is for.

Put them to the principal with **one** `msg.present_principal` whose refs are
**every id in your wake's refs**. The page groups them by what they are about.
Be explicit that these are observations awaiting a first decision. An observed
entry that is challenged forces that decision, which is the point of the
distinction — it is the difference between "the code does this" and "we meant it
to".

One page, not several. Measured (tipsK, 2026-09-09): the rows went out two or
three at a time over eight sessions, and the principal saw the same page
eleven times. Every ref you leave out comes back as another page.
