MODE: blindspot — what this run could not see, said out loud.

`[code.gaps]` is the run's own record of its limits, recomputed: file
formats no parser reads, subjects given up after the attempt bound, prose
nobody checked, claims falsified and awaiting a ruling. The numbers are
mechanical and already true; your job is the judgement of which of them the
principal must know.

Weigh each fact by what it could be hiding. A hundred unreadable fixture
files may witness the project's most important contract; one quarantined
survey may be the area the next question lands on. A fact that hides
nothing — a lockfile no parser reads — is not worth an entry.

For each gap that matters, one entry:
`ledger.log(about_ref=<the path or subject>, about_table="items",
default_taken="this run could not see <what>; <what that could hide>;
proceeding without it")` — two or three real entries beat six restatements
of the same number.

End with `surveys.attest(outcome="found", citations=[a path each entry
names])` when you logged anything, or `outcome="none_found"` when the run's
gaps hide nothing worth a ruling — rare, and say it honestly if true.
