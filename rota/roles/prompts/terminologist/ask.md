`msg.answer_liaison` with refs to the terms.

Say what the glossary holds. If it holds two senses, say two; resolving a

**If your artefact does not carry it, say so** — `msg.report_liaison`. That is a
result: it tells the asker to try the owners who have not spoken, and read-only
sessions cost nothing, so being the wrong owner is free. What is not free is
answering anyway. An answer here is relayed to the principal, so an answer with
nothing behind it spends the one budget in this system that cannot be topped up.
