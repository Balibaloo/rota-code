## 1. The binder gap

### 1.1 The mechanism

`_subject_parts` (api.py:13372) keeps a reference only in three places. The lead place is two words or fewer from the clause start, and `_NOT_SUBJECT` must not match the text before it (13394-13395). The joined place follows the reference before it (13396-13401). The on place follows "on" (13402-13403). Every other reference meets `continue` at 13405 and binds nothing.

`_clause_spans` cuts at `_CUE_CLAUSE` and at a sentence end only. It does not cut at a comma. So one clause can hold two subjects.
