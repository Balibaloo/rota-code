## 2. Design A: the moment

The smallest place is C46's sprint end. It already fires after a read,
already writes the order, and already writes it where every take looks.
Its fault is the trigger word "night" (reasoned). A new section would be a
second writer of the order, against "Every record has one writer"
(composition, Records).

The inputs, all records that exist:

- The run's read page: its walls, its roots, its matched unbuilt frames.
- The Destination line of the last run, at the stack's head.
- The long term plan, `plans/stack.md` 51, for the step and its measure.
- `rota/COMPLETION.md`, "Core, by capability", for the capability names.
- The walls placed under cause 5 of `plans/wall-guide.md` after C36.

The four outputs go in the newest order section at the stack's head, as
four bullets under its dated heading. One writer: the session that holds
the order. Under the Liaison and Steward split, that is the Liaison, on
the wake "a close record waits", because a run's read is the close record
of the run. The Steward or the worker writes the read and never the order
(reasoned: the build-session design, lines 104 to 110).

1. `- Destination:` the run against the destination sentence, and the step
   of the long term plan it reached.
2. `- Order:` the next frames, re-derived from the open frames and the
   read, each with its capability.
3. `- Next:` build or measure, with the reason.
4. `- Model:` the walls the run shows as a model's limit, or "none".

How the next session finds it: the take reads the state print (CLAUDE.md,
"On wake", step 3). Design B prints lines 1 and 3. No new instruction goes
into CLAUDE.md, a memory or a hook (reasoned: "a million and one
reminders").

## 4. The generalisation test

Said for a project that is not rota. The project has a destination
sentence, a list of capabilities, a backlog of work items, a place where
the order stands, and a measurement end to end, such as an acceptance
run, a user trial or a release candidate.

- A, in general words: "After each measurement end to end is read, the
  person who holds the order writes four lines at the head of the order:
  where the measurement stands against the destination, the order
  re-derived from the open items, build or measure next with its reason,
  and where a part the project does not build stops. Build next when the
  destination line repeats, or when the next measurement would first meet
  items that are known and not built." The first three lines survive. The
  Model line survives only where the destination names a limit, as rota's
  does: "Honest about where small models stop". For a project whose
  destination names no limit, cut the Model line. Rota keeps it, because
  its destination names one.
- B, in general words: "The status view counts the open work by the
  capability it serves, and shows the newest destination line and the
  next act beside the count of open items." It survives. It needs one
  field per item that names a capability, and the place of the order.
  Any tracker holds both. The view prints and never refuses.
- Neither design names a night, a dry run, a seat or a door. "Run" means
  any measurement end to end. The `Stuck` rule and the C55 rule are
  general: do not repeat a test whose result is already known.
