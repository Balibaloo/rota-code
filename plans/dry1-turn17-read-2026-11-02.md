## 6. The proposal

The smallest place that covers the whole reach is one end reader for a drop on an observed row, at `land` in principal.py beside `_item_drops` (reasoned):

1. `land` takes every ref that a landed `contest` with the act `drop` names, and that is neither a statement nor a request item, out of the verdict's contest relay. Then no seat sets `contested` and `tick:contested` never fires for it.
2. One helper, `principal_dropped(conn, ref)`, reads the end from `ruled_refs` through the existing `grant(conn, ref, "drop")`. No schema change.
3. Three readers skip a dropped row: `constraints_for_grains`, the account page's `account_page_refs`, and `observed_entries`' limbo list.

Price: 1 cycle for a design page, then 3 cycles, an instrument fix with a review and a gate (reasoned: `plans/operating-facts.md` line 250, "a door text or a number, 2; an instrument fix, 3"). It leaves out the Liaison's over-read of "every note", the false footer, and the side finding on m146.

| Option | Covers | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Present text: no "starts building" when no item is open | The false promise only. He is still asked about 15 notes. | 2 cycles |
| Re-survey rows after a merge to the ledger, not to a page | The whole of turn 17: no page, no drop, no loop. The rows stay visible in the ledger. | 3 cycles |

The fix at the source is the ledger option (reasoned). He did not ask for the notes, so asking him to rule them spends his attention on a question that is not his. The ledger keeps the re-survey visible, so "no work stays invisible" holds. The drop end state fixes the reach that remains on the account page that the 2026-10-28 ruling creates.

Changes that need a design page and Roman's ruling (reasoned):

- The drop end state changes what the act `drop` means on an observed row, in the rulings door family, against the frame 357 review's reason that a dropped description has no way back.
- An exit for the Vision Keeper, "he dropped it and said nothing instead", changes a prompt's shape.
- The ledger option changes the put-once rule of `observed_entries` for a re-survey. That is a predicate change and not a door family, so it needs no ruling unless it removes the account page (reasoned).
