- The "roman_next" classes are mine. MZFI is "yes" because both sentences doubt the build after his question. A stricter reader can call it "partly".

- I hold the key and the current CLAUDE.md, so my own classes are not blind.
- The numbers are small: 14, 7 and 14 stretches, and 10 double reads. I ran no test of significance.
- I took the labels of the key as given. Four of the 14 messages at the cut carry the label "question, correction" or "new ask, correction": CUOV, IDZF, MZFI and JDMR.
