## Result

1. From 2026-10-10 to 2026-10-30 the destination is absent from the acts. Of 157
   frames opened then, 0 Sprint lines name a capability or the destination, and
   6 frame texts name the destination at all. Of 23 sprint orders in that time,
   23 are "recommended on night N's read", and 0 name the destination or a
   capability. Of 2,779 status lines, 6 name the destination.
2. A session in that time knows "done" by a door count on the next nights. Of 157
   frames, 116 close on a count "on a night, from N toward 0 of the next 3". 14
   name a merge in the Ends when. The count measures one door, not the run.
3. The purpose sentence, "no work he did not ask for, no work invisible", is
   present at the door scale. 52 of 168 headings name the principal, his words or
   his request. Frames 243, 306, 357 and 358 are about work he did not ask for.
   The sentence steers what a frame fixes. It does not steer which frame is next.
4. From 2026-10-31 the destination is present, and Roman's words put it there. 7 of
   11 frames opened from 2026-10-31 name a capability in the Sprint line, after
   rule C57 was amended on his question "was it forgotten?". The seat measure
   (changes merged, repositories, turns to an answer) was written six times from
   2026-10-31 to 2026-11-02, and dry runs 1 to 3 merged on three repositories.
5. The seat measure then stops. Dry runs 4 to 8 (2026-11-03 to 2026-11-08) have no
   seat-measure line in `plans/operating-facts.md`. Their reads lead with "did not
   merge" and then count the onboarding slice of frame 362. None of the six run
   reads sampled has a line that says how far the run is from the destination.
6. Roman raised the purpose 18 times in the window. A session raised it alone 5
   times, all from 2026-11-01, each as the reason to place one frame. No session
   stopped work, or said that the work was off the purpose, with no word from him.
   The one session proposal toward the destination (2026-10-18 03:17) was stopped
   by him: "I don't like the direction. Let's just keep working".

## 7. Where the purpose steered the acts

The stretch is 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-03, frames 353 to 360.

- The order came from one run with a seat: "every turn of it finds the next wall in
  the order a person meets them" (handover 393648aa).
- The Ends when included a dry run turn and a merge the seat can read (353, 355, 359).
- The Sprint line named a capability on 7 of the 8 frames 354 to 361, and 357 named the purpose line.
- One measure was written per run, the seat measure, beside the earlier ones.
- The result: one merge on click (11-02 02:32), tips (11-02, 5 turns, 26 minutes) and
  icalendar (11-02 20:27). Handovers 208997ee and ce483c7b lead with that count.

What was different: Roman had just ruled the seat to the session and the plan to a
measure ("multiple changes on multiple repos"). The session sat in the Principal
seat, so the person's view was its own. The unit of work was the run, and a wall
was fixed because the run could not move past it. The stretch ended when Roman asked
about project understanding on 2026-11-03. Frame 362 then held the dry runs, and the
seat measure stopped.

The opposite stretch is 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-29: 23 night reads made sprints,
and frames closed on door counts. The closed-reach read found that of 67 frames
closed from 10-12 to 10-26, 48 fixed one door of N, and 45 of 48 have a later frame
at the same fact.

## 8. What the records do not show

- The terminal replies. The first reply after a take and most questions are in
  transcripts, which this read did not open. Step 5 rests on notes and status lines.
- The reason a session had in mind. A status line can write a reason after the
  choice. A missing word "destination" does not prove a missing thought.
- Which sentence is "the purpose". The purpose sentence is present at the door
  scale and the destination sentence is absent. A reader who takes the purpose
  sentence alone gets a different answer from this page.
- Whether the merges of dry runs 1 to 3 test the destination. The stack head of
  2026-11-10 says the seat had the session's code knowledge. That head summarises the
  meta review, which this read did not open, so the point is not checked here.
- How Roman judges the sessions' reports. His questions show doubt, not the cause.
- The counts in section 2 come from keyword scripts over frame text. A frame can tie
  to a capability in words the scripts do not match. The 20-frame table was read by
  eye and agrees with the script for those 20.
