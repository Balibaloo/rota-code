## The hypotheses

1. Loss. The goal does not cross a boundary between sessions. A note
   carries the next act and not the question.
2. Say-back. A session that must say the goal back in its own words
   shows a difference that a one-way note hides. One more exchange
   closes the difference.
3. Act. A session that holds the question changes its next act. It does
   not only change its words.

## The limits

note or a message. No session here is blind to his question. The test
shows whether a say-back finds a difference. It does not show that a
taker of next week holds the goal.
