No fix, rule or tool changes before step 5.

## The eight masses

1. **Purpose.** What the workflow is for, and how a session knows that it
   serves it.
2. **The work loop.** An issue is spotted, placed, built, measured and
   closed.
3. **How a solution gets its shape** (Roman, 2026-11-10: "how can issues
   be really solved at the appropriate scales. As in how does the workflow
   avoid limiting the correct solution"). Not whether an issue is acted
   on. Whether the workflow lets the solution of the right size exist at
   all (confirmed by Roman: "Yes").
4. **The actors.** The holding session, its agents, the reserve, and Roman.
5. **The records.** The stack, the rules by moment, the memory, the
   handover notes, the read pages.
6. **The instruments.** The state print, the gate, the status tool and the
   hand-off tool.
7. **Continuity.** How one session's understanding reaches the next.
8. **Learning.** How the workflow changes itself: strikes, rules, reviews.

## Step 4, the connections: candidates, not tested

Written by the session from the six reads, 2026-11-10. Each is a claim
for a reader to break. `plans/method/meta-connections-test-2026-11-10.md`
holds the test, running.

**The test's verdicts, 2026-11-10** (`meta-connections-test-2026-11-10.md`):
A holds in part: the frame's form sets a first solution of one door, but
sessions and agents did see the size, on 2026-10-18, on 2026-10-29, in
frame 348's doubt, and when sessions re-priced frames in September. B
holds for signs of size only; signs of fact were read when someone
looked, and a count that adds up does not stop a repeat (the heredoc
lesson, 30 strikes). C holds in part: a line changes an act only when a
choice uses it; 927 Cycles lines changed nothing. D does not hold as a
cause. E does not hold as stated: size and direction were given several
times; what holds is that the one route to a larger solution is a
question to Roman and no session asked it. The connection the session
missed, and the strongest: **the build measures itself by its own
output.** A frame closes on the fix's own counts, the reach count is not
checked, and 32 reds became the gate's baseline. The blind spot of all
six reads: every count comes from the session's records about itself,
which quote Roman word for word and hold none of the session's
reasoning, so "Roman started it" may in part be an effect of what is
recorded. The root "no seat holds the whole" was written before the
reads. Not explained by A to E: the heredoc strikes, the commit footers,
the wrong causes, why the product fails, and Roman's own mixed direction.
Next: a read of the transcripts at moments of choice,
`meta-transcripts-read-2026-11-10.md`, running.

A. **One scale.** The unit, the measure, the moment and the instrument
   are all at the scale of one door in one run. A frame starts from one
   turn. Its end condition counts the fix's own output. A sprint comes
   from one night's read. The session is inside one pass when the size is
   wrong. No actor but Roman stands where the size and the direction show.
B. **The signs of a wrong size exist and have no reader.** A spent price,
   an agent's doubt of the approach, a repeated wall, a repeated lesson, a
   sender's wrong cause. Each gets a new name or a quiet place: a new
   frame, a new memory, a doubt section, a status line. So a repeat never
   adds up.
C. **A line asked for at the moment of an act changes the act. A line
   that describes a state over time goes stale.** The reach line, the
   capability in a Sprint line and the review before a pick work. The
   frame's block, the holders, the order and the print do not, with or
   without a reader. This corrects the session's first guess, that a line
   with a reader stays true.
D. **Writing stands in for acting.** A page, a frame, a rule or a memory
   is the answer to nearly every problem, and most are not read again.
E. **Size and direction were never given to the workflow.** The limits
   are Roman's own rulings, the one route to a larger solution is a
   question to him, no question about size is ever asked, and the one
   proposal to cut the work by the destination was declined.
