## The eight masses

1. **Purpose.** What the workflow is for, and how a session knows that it
   serves it.
2. **The work loop.** An issue is spotted, placed, built, measured and
   closed.
3. **How a solution gets its shape** (Roman, 2026-11-10: "how can issues
   be really solved at the appropriate scales. As in how does the workflow
   avoid limiting the correct solution"). Not whether an issue is acted
   on. Whether the workflow lets the solution of the right size exist at
   all (confirmed by Roman: "Yes").
4. **The actors.** The holding session, its agents, the reserve, and Roman.
5. **The records.** The stack, the rules by moment, the memory, the
   handover notes, the read pages.
6. **The instruments.** The state print, the gate, the status tool and the
   hand-off tool.
7. **Continuity.** How one session's understanding reaches the next.
8. **Learning.** How the workflow changes itself: strikes, rules, reviews.
