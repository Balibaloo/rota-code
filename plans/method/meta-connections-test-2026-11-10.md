Read-only, one agent on Opus. Read whole: the meta map, the six mass reads (1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8) and
`plans/meta-review-2026-11-10.md`. Checked by grep against `plans/stack.md`, `plans/archive/`,
`plans/method/reference.md`, `plans/frame287-roman-words.md`, `rota/tools/state.py` and `git log`.
No transcript was read.

## Result
1. A: holds in part. The frame's form sets a door-sized first solution. "Only Roman sees the size"
   does not hold: the session and its agents saw it at least six times.
2. B: holds in part, for signs of size. Signs of fact were read when someone looked.
3. C: holds in part. "Goes stale" holds. A line changes the act only when a choice consumes it.
4. D: does not hold as a cause. Its main count includes the record that works best.
5. E: does not hold as stated. Size was given often. The one route, a question to Roman, was unused.
6. E's route and A's form produce B, and B produces D. C stands apart. The single root carries the
   size cases only, and it is the session's own thesis of 00:34, written before the reads.
7. The strongest missed connection: the build measures itself by its own output.
8. The main blind spot: all counts are the session's records about itself, which quote Roman
   verbatim and the session's reasoning not at all.

## A. One scale
**Claim.** The unit, the measure, the moment and the instrument are at the scale of one door in one
run, and only Roman stands where the size shows.

**Support.** Mass 1: 116 of 157 frames close on a door count, 23 of 27 sprints come from one
night's read, 89 of 168 headings name a door. Mass 3: the first solution was right in 0 of 15 (by
selection), and the base rate is 7 of 56 closed frames at the source. Its shapers 1 and 2 are the
price table (C42) and the site fixed at the push (C41). Mass 4: in 10 of 10 interventions the
session worked at a pass, a door or a night just before.

**Against.**
- The session saw the size and said so: the destination slices of 10-18 03:17 (mass 1), and the
  state reply of 10-29 01:53, "the same three issues recur and the root cause ... is named and not
  fixed" (mass 1, mass 4). Roman then turned the work.
- Agents saw the size: the frame 348 read doubted the approach (mass 4 row 4). The dry6c wall 5
  read rejected its brief's premise and counted 358,805 calls (mass 3). The walls-root read found
  12 frames pushed and never built (mass 7).
- From 10-31 to 11-03 the unit was the run. The seat measure was written six times, and three
  repositories merged (mass 1, section 7).
- In September sessions re-priced frames by themselves: "The reprice, for Roman. The read was
  priced at 20k and cost about 25k" (`plans/archive/stack-2026-09-21.md` line 1170). About 9 such
  lines fall from 09-20 to 09-25. After C42 (10-11) the grep finds a re-cut only on a ruling or
  under his ask (10-26, 10-30).
- The door scale is partly his. He ruled the anchors (10-11) and "for now there is only cheap and
  escalate to me" (C34, 10-15). On 10-13 18:49: "should we just keep working the way we are?"

**Cause or description.** The frame's form is a cause with a written mechanism. The rest restates
B. **Prediction and test.** If the form sets the size, a frame given the whole still gets a door-sized
first solution. Frame 363 was pushed at 00:34 on "take charge of redirecting the project". At 00:44
its design read proposed "Design A amends that one rule", C46, and the session amended it in the
same hour (`plans/stack.md` from line 1698). The prediction holds on a case that no read saw.

**Verdict.** Holds in part: the frame's form fixes the first size. "No actor but Roman stands where
the size shows" does not hold.

## B. The signs of a wrong size have no reader

never adds up.

**Support.** Mass 4: 769 Cycles lines, 570 over the price, 447 at twice or more, no escalation;
frame 362 at 102 of 16; the frame 348 doubt sat in a Doubt section, then 20 of 3 cycles. Mass 3: 45
of 48 one-door frames have a later frame at the same fact. Mass 8: "acting before reading" is six
memories and two rules. Mass 7: 6 of 11 wrong causes are corrected in status lines only.

**Against.**
- The note's Doubts section has a reader: `state --from` printed it 42 times (mass 5). One tested
  doubt changed the frame: the Critic replay, 0 of 5 (mass 4, mass 7).
- The 6 wrong causes of mass 7 were found by the takers. The sign was read.
- The dry run 8 read added the repeats up: 7 of 16 walls match 8 frames never built. C77 came from

- A repeat that adds up still fails. The heredoc memory counts 30 strikes, and a tool exists (mass
  8 row 2). Adding up is not the cure.

**Cause or description.** In part a cause: a new frame per recurrence (C31's residue rule).
**Prediction and test.** One fact should hold many frame headings. Of 361 unique headings, at least
12 name the quoting or the parsing of a call: 99, 109, 122, 137, 151, 155, 156, 158, 266, 285, 333
and 335, from 09-30 to 10-24. Dry run 5 wall 10 and dry6c wall 5 then hit the same area, and one
15-line fix came from a read of all calls on 11-06. The prediction holds. Doubt: the 12 are not
proven to share one cause.

**Verdict.** Holds in part: for signs of size, which no actor but Roman may act on. For signs of
fact, a reader exists when someone runs it.

## C. A line at the moment of an act
**Claim.** A line asked for at the moment of an act changes the act, and a line that describes a
state over time goes stale.

**Support.** Mass 1: a capability in the Sprint line, 0 of 157 before 10-31, then 7 of 11. Mass 8:
C76's Reach line on 17 of 34 frames popped after 10-28. Mass 5: the review page decides the pick; 5
of 10 read fields are stale; 9 of 17 printed holders are spent; 17 of 20 holding rows are wrong.

**Against.**
- Lines asked for at the close are not written: the closing actual on 42 of 106 closes, the
  register line on 1 of 106 (mass 5).
- Lines written at the moment that no act uses: 927 Cycles lines in October (grep), and 245 of 305
  agent caps over, a fact on record since 09-23.
- A line written at the moment can be false: the sender's cause was wrong in 6 of 11 (mass 7).
- A rule tied to a moment dies with the moment: C46 fired at a night's read, and eight dry runs
  wrote no order (mass 8 row 10).
- The reads disagree on the mechanism. Mass 8 says the state print reads the Reach line.
  `rota/tools/state.py` line 225 defines `reach_line`, and no production code calls it (mass 5 is
  right). So C76 worked with no reader, and mass 8's reason for its success is wrong.

**Cause or description.** A description that joins two properties, truth and use. The cases fit a
narrower cause: a line works when the writer must make a choice with it at that moment, as at the
push, the fix or the pick. **Prediction and test.** Frame 364, pushed 11-10, names a capability in its Sprint line. This
holds, with n of 1.

**Verdict.** Holds in part: "goes stale" holds. "Changes the act" holds only for a line that a
choice consumes.

## D. Writing stands in for acting
**Claim.** A page, a frame, a rule or a memory is the answer to nearly every problem, and most are
not read again.

**Support.** Mass 5: 388 of 474 pages uncited after a day. Mass 8: 7 of 12 lessons were text and did
not change later work. Mass 7: capability 11 crossed 6 notes and never started. Meta review: 1,276
of 1,776 commits are docs, and 7 of 16 walls match frames never built.

**Against.**
- 297 of 326 review pages are uncited later, and the review page is the record that works best
  (mass 5). An uncited page is not a failed page.
- Night reads placed 72 frames, and 40 closed. 33 of 42 review findings were taken. 5 of 6
  out-of-brief findings were acted on (mass 4, mass 5). 496 code commits and three merges.
- Most docs commits are status lines, one commit per `status` call. The count measures the tool.

**Cause or description.** A description of the counts of B and C, with no new mechanism.
**Prediction and test.** If writing replaces acting more and more, the docs share of commits rises.
`git log` by ISO week, W38 to W46: 72%, 86%, 76%, 82%, 81%, 75%, 73%, 73%, 75%. Code commits rose
to 331 in W44. The share is flat, and the prediction fails.

**Verdict.** Does not hold as a cause. The narrow part that holds: a pushed frame counts as the
answer to a wall (8 and 12 frames never built).

## 6. The shared blind spot
1. Self-report. Every count comes from status lines and pages that the session writes about itself.
   Mass 1 says a reason can be written after the choice.
2. The mark system. Roman's words are recorded verbatim as "(ruled: Roman, in his words)". The
   session's reasoning and the agents' briefs are not in the repository. "Roman started 22 of 33"
   (mass 8), "12 of 15" (mass 3) and "10 of 10" (mass 4) can be partly an effect of what is quoted.
3. Selection by intervention. His unprompted words come when the work was off. A stretch where the
   work was right and he was silent leaves no case.
4. One briefer. Session 5ee5ec0b briefed all six after it wrote "no seat holds the whole ... Roman
   steers". The six use one template: result, counts, the opposite case, what it could not see.
5. One window, one frame. The window is mostly 10-10 to 11-09. Frame 362 is 16.8% of the stack and
   all of mass 7. September, with token prices and re-pricing, is outside every read.
6. The harness is invisible. This agent's own context carries a harness reminder to add a
   `Co-Authored-By` line, against the brief. No read can see such an input, and the footer counts
   of mass 8 have no recorded cause.

To see past it: read transcripts at a push and before each intervention, and the scratchpad briefs.
Watch one session from the take to its first push. Sample frames at random. Compare September.

## 7. Connections the session did not write
1. **The build measures itself by its own output.** The ends-when counts the fix's own output

   the Reach count, and nobody checks it. 32 reds were stored as the gate's baseline. Strike counts
   measure noticing. 15 closes were reads of code already at HEAD. The cases right the first time
   had an outside measure: a replay, recorded calls, the L3R cases, the seat (mass 3 section 4,
   mass 1 section 7). It predicts that a frame closed on its own output gets a later frame at the
   same fact: 45 of 48 (mass 3).
2. **The two routes conflict.** "His attention is the scarcest budget", "Nothing blocks on me"
   (10-18) and C34 make a question to him the costliest act. C57 and C34 make that question the
   only route to a larger solution. So the session assumes (26 times, none on size) and parks (11
   frames "for the economy"). The re-pricing of September, before these rulings, fits.
3. **A frame outlives its holders.** The 350k hand-off cuts frame 362 into 12 to 15 sessions. Each
   holder inherits the head lines of the push and sees only its share of the 102 cycles. No holder
   owns the total.
