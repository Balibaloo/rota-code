# Mass 7: how one session's understanding reaches the next, and what is lost

A blind read of the records, 2026-11-10. The sample is the chain of frame 362, 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-10: 12 sessions and 11 hand-offs. Six notes were read whole (ce483c7b, 07f209e6, 535a30c6, 9f60e445, 94cb1260, 1b9e813c), and the five later notes were read whole for the item trace.

## Result

1. The next step crosses. In 10 of 11 hand-offs the taker started the note's first item. The median time from the take to the first new agent is about 3 minutes.
2. The latest causes cross, and they are often wrong. In 6 of 11 hand-offs the taker found, within hours, that a cause the sender had recorded was wrong.
3. Doubts cross as text, but few are tested. Of 10 items followed from the earliest note, 2 were acted on whole, 3 in part, 2 were copied forward with no act, and 3 were dropped with no word.
4. The dialogue of the rules is almost absent. 0 of 11 hand-offs record a question from the taker with an answer from the sender. 2 record one message from the sender, and 3 record a release.
5. The whole does not cross. No note after the first cites the long term plan or the destination. The frame's head is unchanged since its push on 2026-11-03 00:39. No sprint section was written in the window.
6. Understanding crossed best when the sender turned it into a rule on a shared page and a test the taker could run. That happened twice: capability 11 (2026-11-04) and C77 (2026-11-08).

## 6. The opposite case: the taker started from the sender's understanding and went further

0d047fa7 to 0ff6bcd7, 2026-11-08. Roman asked at 10:2x: "we are finding more walls, are we doing proper root cause analysis?" Before the hand-off the sender did three things. It wrote rule C77 into `plans/method/reference.md` (10:25). It wrote item 0 of the note as a hypothesis with its test: "the three biggest walls share one root, a thing with no row is invisible to every seat ... If the hypothesis holds, the fix is at the reading ... Also count how many of the ten walls match a frame that was pushed and never built." It put the hypothesis before every build.

The taker launched the read at the take (10:30). At 10:44 it had 20 walls under four roots. The hypothesis "holds on the three biggest walls and fails as a general law". 12 frames were pushed and never built. "The reading that turns his words into rows" was "the wrong place: right for 2 of 6 R1 walls", and the general place was the "rests on" edge. Slice 2 was built and gated by 14:14. The next taker used C77 again: "Frames 182 and 171 moved to the front by C77".

What was different: Roman's words were in the note, the method was in a shared rule, and the sender's belief was written as a claim with its own test. The taker did not have to find what the sender believed. It had to check it.

A second, smaller case: 1b9e813c wrote "Backslashes are a small part of why the Tester's tests do not land, and the larger part is unread." The taker read that part at 08:11 and found two doors that refused 30 of 102 good tests. An explicit "unread" became the next read.

## 7. What the records do not show

- The transcripts. What each taker read at the take, in what order, and at what token cost.
- The messages. The harness does not log them in the repository. Only `released` events and status lines show that a message passed.
- Whether Roman spoke to a session outside the stack.
- The briefs and the reports in the scratchpads, which this read did not open.
- How 535a30c6 found the addenda of the older note.
- Whether "Rota itself failed 2 times" on dry run 3 had the cp1252 cause.
- Blindness: the state print showed the headings of frames 363 and 364 of 2026-11-10. This read saw those two lines and no other part of them.

## The main doubt

The 6 corrections can be a gain and not a loss. A clean context finds a wrong cause that a full context wrote. The records cannot separate a sender that knew and did not write from a sender that believed a wrong cause. The sample is one chain, on one request on one repository, and other chains can differ.
