# Who does what, who sees what, who can say "wrong": a blind read, 2026-11-10

Read-only, by one agent, from the rule pages, the stack (not lines 17 to 75), the archives of 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-08, 38 hand-off notes, and 18 report pages under `plans/`. No meta page of 2026-11-10 and no `f363-*` page was read. The period is 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-09.

## Result

1. Judgement about the size and the direction of the work sits with Roman alone. In 10 of 10 of his unprompted interventions, his words changed the work, and 7 of the 10 were about the size of a fix or the direction of the build (observed: section 5). In the same period, no agent and no session stopped, re-cut or dropped a frame for its size, and no question went to Roman on a spent budget (observed: section 7).
2. Judgement about the shape inside a frame sits with the agents and the session, and it works there. 6 of 12 agent reports said something outside the brief at the scale of the frame, and the session acted on 5 of the 6 (observed: section 2). Of 42 findings in 6 review pages, the session took 33 (observed: section 3).
3. Judgement is lost in two places. First, an agent's doubt about the approach of a frame goes into a "Doubt" section that no rule reads: frame 348's read said its fix was an unruled word list, and the frame then ran 8 passes at 20 cycles of 3 priced. Second, the price of a frame is a running count that every taker inherits and no rule reads: frame 362 had 12 holding sessions and reached 102 cycles of 16 priced.
4. Three things can say "this is wrong" and be acted on: Roman always, an agent when the session judges its finding at the frame's scale, and a sender's doubt when the taker tests it. No actor can say "this frame is too big" or "this is the wrong frame" and be acted on, except Roman.
5. No actor sees the whole and is not the owner. Four whole-view reads ran in the period, and Roman's words started all 4 (observed: section 7). The design of 2026-10-29 proposed a seat for that view, the Liaison over a Steward. The records show only one part built, the design moment. "Steward" appears in no rule page, in no stack line and in no amendment (observed: grep, 2026-11-10).

## 6. Between sessions

The rules give one channel at one time. A sender writes a note with "what to doubt first" (C10). A taker may message the sender "for a context question" (C10, M13). One session holds the stack (C11). "Never spend a standby's context ... do not tell it what changed" (CLAUDE.md). A message "is never the first route and never the only route" (C7). No rule lets a session tell a live session that it does the wrong thing, and two live sessions never hold one stack.
