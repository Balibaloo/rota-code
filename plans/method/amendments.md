# Proposed amendments

Meta workflow. A session that wants a change to `plans/method/intent.md`
or to a rule in `plans/method/reference.md` writes it here, dated, with
its reason. Roman reads the page when he returns and rules each line. A
ruled line moves to the page it amends, with its mark, and leaves this
page. An amendment proposed here changes nothing until he rules
(reasoned: `plans/method/design.md`, section 2, the drift guard).

Form: `- <date> <page>: <the change> (reasoned: <why>). Recommended: <accept or decline>.`
