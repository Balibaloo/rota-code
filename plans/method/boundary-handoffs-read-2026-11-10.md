## Method

1. The list of hand-offs comes from the 151 receipts in `.handover/taken/`. A receipt names the taker, the sender (`from_sid`), the frame and the time the claim was posted.
2. The take time is the `taken` event of `.handover/log.jsonl` for the receipt. 28 of 151 takes came more than 60 seconds after the post, because the claim waited as a vacancy.
3. A hand-off is in the list when the note `handoff-<sender>.md` exists under `plans/method/` or `plans/` and the taker's transcript exists. That gives 93.
4. The draw sorts the 93 by time and runs `random.seed(20261110)` and `random.sample(list, 30)`. The 2 hand-offs of 2026-11-10 that the draw missed are added. No hand-off was taken on 2026-11-09.
5. The note version is the last git commit of the note at or before the post time plus 120 seconds.
6. The window is the take time to three hours after it, in local time.
7. The first act counts the tools Edit, Write, NotebookEdit and Agent, and Bash or PowerShell with `git commit` in the command.
8. A message counts from the taker to the sender when its text appears as a received message in the sender's transcript, or when its address is a peer name of the sender from the note's title (H14 only). A message counts from the sender to the taker by the same text match in the other direction, or by the `from-name` of the received message.
9. A typed message is a `user` entry of text that is not a tool result, not `isMeta`, and does not start with the excluded prefixes of the brief, `<local-command`, `[Request interrupted` or `Caveat:`. A slash command counts as typed. None fell in a window.
10. One reader gave the labels and the yes or no facts of step 2. A goal is a sentence that names what the frame or the sprint is for, or the question that the frame exists to answer. A measure of one pass and a doubt do not count. A quote of Roman is a text in quote marks that the note gives as his words. Words of a dry run's principal seat or of a recorded test case do not count.
11. A message that tells the session to pause or stop for a machine reason has the labels "correction, new ask". There are 5: H10 at 63.0, H16 at 89.9 and 90.8, H21 at 21.0, H27 at 161.6. The third column of the correction table leaves them out.
