## The whole, in one paragraph

The build session takes each problem in as a small piece of work at one
place. It takes every problem in, and it lets none go. It starts the
next step at once, because the words that push it are wired and the
words that slow it are prose. It writes true records of each step, and
those records lose the doubts. Only the smallest unit, the frame, has an
end. So three things are missing from the way of working, and Roman
gives all three by hand, about five times a day: the reach of a
solution, the end of a direction, and the brake. The work does raise the
count that it watches. That count is not the destination.

## The eight findings

### 1. Work is cut small, and it stays small

A run's read becomes a list of problems, and each problem becomes one
frame at one place. The price, the brief, the measure and the close
then take that place.

- The first solution was the right size in 0 of 15 cases (the read of
  mass 3). 14 of 19 changes to rota were one door, one refusal text or

### 2. Every problem comes in, and none goes out

- The stack holds 136 open frames. 88 are silent. The limit of 10 live

- The newest run sets the order, in 9 of 12 placements (mass 2). So an

### 3. Only a frame has an end

## How the findings make one loop

1. A run ends. Its read is a list of problems.
2. Each problem becomes a small frame (finding 1), and all of them come
   in (finding 2).
3. The session starts the first one at once (finding 6). The newest run
   set the order, so the older frames go silent.
4. The fix meets its own count at its own place, and the frame closes.
   The line that records it is true and drops the doubt (finding 5).
5. The problem returns in another shape, as a new frame. Nothing joins
   it to the first one (findings 2 and 3).
6. Roman sees the pattern from outside. He corrects the reach, or he
   turns the work (finding 1).
7. The session agrees at once and writes his sentence as a rule in the
   same turn, 10 of 40 moments. No act stands behind the rule (finding
   4). It does not say what the new work ends (finding 3).
8. The session nears its context line and hands off the next step
   (finding 7). The direction fades. He gives it again.

The loop does not need a weak session. Each step in it is correct by
the rule of that step.
