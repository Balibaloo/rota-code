# The composition

Status: ratified by Roman, 2026-09-16. Low resolution on purpose. LAWS
derives from this page. This page does not derive from LAWS.

## Purpose

Rota exists so that no work happens that the Principal did not ask for,
and no work stays invisible.

The destination, agreed 2026-09-10 and held as the bottom frame of the
stack: a careful person runs rota alone on a small Python repository and
gets a merged change they can read. Honest about where small models
stop.

## The parties

**The Principal** is in the team. They hold intent and shape. They rule.
They declare finished. They write no records. They speak, and the Liaison
records. They see everything at a strategic altitude. They can
interrogate any detail through the Liaison. Their attention is the
scarcest budget. Approval of intent is required before work starts.
Sign-offs after that, including on merges, are opt-in.

**The Liaison** is the one seat that faces the Principal. It carries
words both ways. It infers intent, then checks and clarifies with the
Principal. The wiring records the words verbatim before the Liaison
wakes. The Liaison's confirmed reading is a second record. It judges the
route: which question next and what to show. It may originate a question
the Principal did not raise, and the question names the row it is about.
The wiring decides whom an inquiry reaches.

**The structure** is the agent. It encodes the workflow, holds the
records, and decides what happens next. Capability comes from wiring. No
brief grants what is not wired.

**A model** is a component. Each seat wakes cold, acts once, and ends.
Nothing accumulates in a conversation. Everything accumulates in records.

**The project** is the codebase the work is about. The structure holds a
model of its shape, terms, boundaries, and external commitments, as
records observed from the code. The Principal holds the shape in their
head. The structure holds it in rows.

## Records

- Every record has one writer.
- Conclusions cross a boundary. Reasoning stays where it was made,
  reachable by a pointer.
- Nothing stops quietly. Every open obligation stays visible and
  countable until something discharges it.
- Work is not assigned. Being woken is the assignment. Outstanding work
  is re-derived from the records each pass. The unit per pass is a
  question.
- The built unit is a set of tasks formed once, then built, judged, and
  merged whole.
- A seat restarts from records. There is no snapshot.
- A record says whether it was observed from the code or the world,
  reasoned by a seat, or decided by the Principal (ratified 2026-09-16).
- A record's identity derives from its content, not from when it was
  made.
- A ruling is a record. The Principal rules, the Liaison writes it, and
  it carries a version.
- A session commits or discards before it ends, so its output stays
  attributable.

## Flows

- Intent flows down: Principal, Liaison, records, seats.
- Evidence flows up the same path.
- A question changes nothing. Only a ratified amendment to something
  approved revokes work downstream.
- A prediction never blocks. Only a fact or a ruling gates.
- The tool never rules for the Principal. It never settles a conflict,
  closes an assumption, or treats silence as consent.
- Everything from outside the project enters through one seat, as cited
  records.
- Every claim cites a source. A seat can check the claim against the
  source.

## The ceiling

The feedback rate is the ceiling. A weak model is trusted only as far as
the loop that checks it is short. A capability is judged by the loop it
shortens.

## Capabilities

A capability is a way to move intent down or evidence up. The list is the
next resolution, not this page.

## The build session

The same composition applies to the session that builds rota, as a
second instance with its own rules. The assistant sits above rota: one
strong model, one context, no wiring. Its brief is the first section of
`CLAUDE.md` in this repository. Its records are this page, the stack,
and the memory directory.
