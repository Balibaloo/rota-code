## The mark on 6146

finding 79 in `plans/operating-facts.md`: two server slots split
`num_ctx` and ollama cuts the prompt from the front, so the brief is
lost first.

The evidence against that reading (observed: the environment and

its recent slot lines read `new slot, n_ctx = 12288`, and its only seven

from nights 83, 84 or 85.

`rota/core/runner.py:1752` prints `completion.prompt_tokens`, which
`rota/llm/llm.py:402` reads from ollama's `prompt_eval_count`. Whether
6146 is a real halved window or a misreported count is not settled here.
A reviewer holds that question, cap 60k.

What is settled, and needs no model number (observed: the character
counts against the window): a prompt of 64381 characters cannot fit a
12288-token window. English text runs near four characters to the token.
The page is about sixteen thousand tokens.
