## The mark on 6146

6146 is exactly half of 12288, plus two. That is the signature of
finding 79 in `plans/operating-facts.md`: two server slots split
`num_ctx` and ollama cuts the prompt from the front, so the brief is
lost first.

The evidence against that reading (observed: the environment and
`C:/Users/roman/AppData/Local/Ollama/server.log`): the user environment
carries `OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL=1`, the log carries `OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL:1`,
its recent slot lines read `new slot, n_ctx = 12288`, and its only seven

from nights 83, 84 or 85.

A reviewer settled it on 2026-09-18, cap 60k, 88k on the harness line,
70 tool calls. **Neither reading was right. 6146 is measured, and the
window is not split by slots.** Ollama collapses any prompt that
exceeds `num_ctx` to `num_ctx/2 + 2`, and it keeps the tail (observed:
the reviewer replayed s110's prompt with a nonce against both servers,
17220 tokens evaluated at num_ctx 40960, 6146 at 12288, 4098 at 8192;
an 8415-token prompt at 12288 was not cut; a control prompt with a
unique instruction first and a unique fact last returned 6146 and
answered from the fact).

Finding 79's observable was right and its cause was wrong. The
correction is in `plans/operating-facts.md`. The planning budget is
`num_ctx/2`, not `num_ctx`.

The measured sizes of s110's turn one (observed: the reviewer's

The fault is the missing area check, not the signoff.

Five high, ranked.

1. **`_fit` never trims a transcript of fewer than four blocks**

   < 4`; the reviewer called it with one, three and four blocks and only

   `rota/llm/llm.py:404-408` states the wrong cause.
3. **The landing wake carries the same 79 rows twice** (observed: the
   wake JSON is 64294 chars, of which `resolved_refs` is 21402 and
   `landing.line_rows` is 21402, byte-identical). Dropping one cuts the

4. **The present page has no size bound at any hop** (observed:
   `rota/core/predicates.py:653-656` sends every offered row, 79 on

   library. A bigger repository scales this linearly.
5. **`ref_resolves` accepts any `@` string**, as above.

Two lows: the `truncated` flag fires on a character estimate that
over-reports by 13 per cent, and three sessions called
`msg.present_principal` with a tick name nine times, all refused, so the

## What fits, measured

The reviewer measured each candidate against the 12288 window
(observed: the tokeniser probes).

- Drop the duplicate rows: 17220 to 11337 tokens. Breaks nothing.
  Necessary and not sufficient, because a reply needs 567 tokens and
  qwen3 thinking needs room.
- Also build the wake from the page text and the line map, and let the
  seat read rows on demand: **5438 tokens, and it fits**. It breaks
  `landing.md:15`, which tells the seat to answer from the page and
  `line_rows`, and it needs a read tool added to `landing.tools`.
- Page the present, N lines at a time: bounded by construction and the
  correct long-term shape. It breaks the tipsK ruling and
  `observed_entries.md:14-16`, "One page, not several", and the `asked`
  guard at `rota/core/predicates.py:648-651` would have to count one
  page-set as one ask. **This needs Roman's ruling.**
- A per-page default verdict: forbidden. The principal would rule lines
  they did not read.
- A bigger window: does not fix it. The budget is half the window on
  overflow, and the page grows with the repository.
