## 7. Open questions for the design review

1. Does a finding on a constraint the Critic falsified reach the Critic, or
   does the tool dismiss it with no session? Recommended: it reaches the
   Critic, and `findings.load` in the new mode shows the challenge verdict
   beside the finding. A mechanical dismissal is not a fresh verdict.
2. Where does the Critic's ruling live? Recommended: the new table
   `finding_rulings`. A `verdicts` row overwrites the pass on the same commit,
   and `failed_criterion` cannot name a constraint.
3. Does the Architect's `escalate` mode keep `findings.find` to flip a finding
   to `satisfied`? Recommended: keep it. The Developer can still escalate
   after an `upheld` ruling, and that route needs no change now.
4. Does `findings.find` keep overwriting a row by id across commits?
   Recommended: yes for this frame, and record the lost `aeef7c0c` finding as
   a separate question. A ruling keyed by `finding_id` and `commit_sha` still
   reads correctly after the overwrite.
5. Does the fixture sentence change in this frame? Recommended: no. It is a
   one-line change with 0 moved cases, so it is its own frame, and frame 88
   stays on the route.
