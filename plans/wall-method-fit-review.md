## 0. Verdict

**Run the method with a change in what it is for.** The loop is the right
way to fix one wall. It is the wrong thing to fill a sprint with. The
project pushes about ten walls a day and closes about five, the stack holds
100 open frames, and a close names its own residue as the next frame. A
per-wall loop cannot make that count fall, at any price per wall. So the
method should run on the walls that one ratio names, merges that carry
what the principal declined, and the sprint's design frame should be the
judge at the merge gate, a check that reads his verbatim words against the
diff. The other open frames are parked until a night shows one of them on
that ratio. Sections 1 to 6 carry the numbers and the alternatives.

## 2. Convergence

**The price a wall actually costs.** Fourteen closed October frames carry
a "Cycles: actual" line (observed: `plans/archive/stack-2026-10-*.md`):
232 at 4 of 3; 213 at 10 of 4; 226 at 5 of 2; 235 at 1 of 1; 225 at 10 of
2; 221 at 7 of 4; 234 at 12 of 3; 240 at 5 of 2; 238 at 4 of 3; 237 at 12
of 2; 233 at 13 of 8 to 10; 229 at 3 of 3; 228 at 0 of 3; 219 at 3 of 3.
The sum is about 89 cycles against about 43 priced. The median is 5. The
design frames cost more: frame 200 at 44 cycles, frame 204 at 43 against
4, frame 215 at 10 against 4 (observed: the day read, section 5). The
agents of October spent about 57.6M tokens on 499 runs by the harness
lines in the archives and the stack, about 0.8M a closed frame (observed:
a sum of "k on the harness line" over those files, 2026-10-15).

**The price the method names.** A door frame with the collapse is 2 to 3
cycles. A seat wall with a text move is 4 to 5. A sprint holds about
twenty (observed: `plans/wall-method-review-2.md` section 6; the anchor of
2026-10-11 in `plans/operating-facts.md`). Frame 243, the one frame run in
the method's order, stands at six cycles with pass 1b, a review and the
night still to come, so seven or eight (observed: frame 243's status lines
of 2026-10-15). That is the top of the method's range, on its first run.

**The arithmetic.** At the method's own prices a sprint of twenty cycles
closes four to seven walls, and fewer with a design frame in it. At the
observed median of five it closes three or four. A night finds three to
seven, and the push rate is ten a day. So under the method the count of
open walls stays flat at best, and it stays flat only if every wall is a
door frame (reasoned: the three rates above). The method is a loop per
wall. It says nothing about how many walls a night finds, and that number
is what decides convergence. The design's own placement table shows the
same: it "describes the closing fixes" and "does not show that the test
chooses a fix before the pass" (observed: `plans/wall-method-design.md`,
section 6.2, the honest count). A method that chooses fixes well does not
change a wall supply of ten a day.

**What would make it converge.** The count falls when a close removes the
cause of several walls at once. The record holds two such closes. Frame
131, a test calls a plain function as a Click command, was put first on
2026-10-07 "because every night since 120 ends on its shape", after "24
frames closed in five days against 32 pushed in two" (observed: the ruling
of 2026-10-07 00:55). Frame 190, what the principal sees, took frames 45,
184 to 189 and three nights' walls as one design, after "the frames pushed
since 2026-10-08 are each one symptom of that fact" (observed: the ruling
of 2026-10-09 10:31). Both were a design frame on the shared cause, ruled
over the per-wall order. The wall method has no step for that choice. Its
step 1 says "push one frame per wall" (observed: the block in `CLAUDE.md`).

## 3. Fixed models, adapting structure

**The assumption is ruled and measured, on one kind of wall.** The
composition's ceiling says "A weak model is trusted only as far as the
loop that checks it is short. A capability is judged by the loop it
shortens" (observed: `plans/composition.md`, The ceiling). The destination
says "Honest about where small models stop". The card fits models of 5.2
GB and under at a 12k window, `qwen3:8b` is the engine, the 14B runs at
153 s a turn at 75 per cent on the card, and the 27B models overflow by 8
GB (observed: `plans/operating-facts.md`, Models and Ollama). Roman's rule
is "the model's capability is sound until disproven. The model is the last
suspect" (observed: the same file, the rulings on briefs and doors,
2026-09-14/15). So a fixed seat model with a structure that adapts is the
project's own line, by the destination and by the hardware.

**What frame 75 measured.** Through the runner's own `complete()`, with a
prompt whose repeat line pointed at an evicted source, every local model
wrote `confirm()` from memory and committed it: `qwen3.5:9b` in 28 s,
`qwen2.5:14b` byte for byte with the 9B for 799 characters in 464 s,
`gemma3:12b` with an invented signature, `mistral-nemo:12b` after a
paragraph of prose. "A larger model does not close the hole, so the fault
is the structure's by Roman's own criterion" (observed:
`plans/archive/stack-2026-09-23.md`, frame 75, 2026-09-23 15:20). That is
one trial, on an instrument fault: the structure told every model that an
answer was above when it was not. It says that a stronger model does not
repair a false prompt. It says nothing about a judgement wall, a seat that
had the right facts and read the principal's words wrong (reasoned: the
trial's prompt held the fault).

**The walls of this month are judgement walls on his words.** Frames 221,
234, 243, 250, 219, 202, 213, 226 and 227 are each a seat that rewrote,
contested or superseded what the principal said. The design's own cost
table prices "the principal's words" at 7 to 10 cycles a wall, its most
expensive row (observed: `plans/wall-method-design.md` section 2, the cost
table). Frame 243's interview found s188 "did not read the words as a
rejection" with the sentence already in its brief (observed: frame 243's
status of 01:37). The design marks move H, a stronger model for one step,
as "a move with a measured miss, not an untried one", from frame 75 alone
(observed: section 2). That mark is a copied claim on the wrong kind of
wall. The wiring for a model per wake kind exists at
`rota/core/config.py:244` (observed: the design, section 2). The Critic
measured once that "a different model, not a bigger model, was the lever"
(observed: `plans/operating-facts.md`, the Critic's lever, 2026-09-12).

**What would change.** A stronger model on one step is a replay with
`--model`, priced at one cycle, on the recorded turns of s188, s173, s130
and the m81 verdict. If the 14B reads "It should not expect only the
command name and the Python version" as a rejection where the 8B does
not, the project knows where small models stop on his words, which the
destination asks it to be honest about. If it does not, frame 75's claim
gains a second trial on the right wall. Either way the assumption stays.
The method should hold H as a one-cycle replay on a judgement wall, and
not as the last rung after three failed moves (reasoned: the cost of the
trial against the 7 to 10 cycles of the row it tests).

## 4. Three alternatives, priced

The method as ruled spends 2 to 8 cycles and a night per wall, at a seam
chosen per wall. Each alternative below is priced against that.

### 4.1 The judge at the merge gate

What it is. One check before a merge reads the principal's verbatim words
on the request, the rulings and the declines, against the diff and the
tests. The executable part: a declined phrase that appears in the diff, a
test in a file his words forbade. The judgement part: a Critic pass that
cites his words beside the criterion it fails. A merge that carries what he
declined fails with his sentence named. The parts exist in pieces: frame
170 made the Critic cite the criterion's words, 4 of 6 lines on a night;
frame 213 put `principal_said` on the Tester's wake, 3 of 5; frame 243's
pass 1 puts every contest ruling's words in the Developer's and Tester's
wake (observed: the three frames' close and status lines).

What it buys. One place catches every "merged what he declined" wall,
whatever its seam. Frame 243 found three seams for one effect on one
night, and the method is fixing them as three frames, 243, 250 and 244
(observed: frame 243's status of 01:33; sprint 6 rows 1, 3 and the frame
250 heading). The judge turns that class from a wall per seam into one
ratio: merges that carry what he declined, from 1 of 3 toward 0. It also
answers the destination's "honest about where small models stop": a merge
that fails with his sentence named is a change a careful person can read.

What it costs. A seam design with its night, 8 to 10 cycles (observed: the
anchor of 2026-10-11). The Critic half is a 9B judgement, so its ratio is
stochastic and the three-part ends-when applies. Roman's attention: none
per merge. Rulings crossed: none. Law 8 names the merge gate "harness,
then Critic, then Architect" (observed: `rota/LAWS.md`, law 8). The
composition says "Every claim cites a source", and "The tool never rules
for the Principal": a check that applies his recorded sentence is not a
ruling, it is a citation (reasoned: the words of Flows).

### 4.2 A principal checkpoint after every rewrite of his words

What it is. After a contest ruling, the seat's rewrite of the criterion
goes to his page as one line, "is this what you said", before any build
runs on it. A yes-only agent principal replays it on a night when the page
text matches a recording (observed: frame 233's close, the principal's
answer replay).

What it buys. The composition's own line: the Liaison "infers intent,
then checks and clarifies with the Principal". s188, s189, s130 and s173
would each have met his "no" before s190 built (reasoned: the chain in
frame 243's Site line).

What it costs. 2 to 3 cycles, a predicate and a page line, and a night. His
attention: two to four more asks a night on top of 12 to 25 (observed: the
respecify counts in frame 243's status of 01:33; the ask counts in
`plans/night148-read.md` 13.3), and on a real run one minute of his per
rewrite. Rulings crossed: "Approval of intent is required before work
starts. Sign-offs after that, including on merges, are opt-in" (observed:
`plans/composition.md`, The parties), and law 7, where `interrupt_cap`
"spends the principal". So it must be an opt-in setting beside
`merge_gate`, never the default. It is the cheapest of the three, and it
spends the scarcest budget.

### 4.3 Fewer seats on the path his words take

What it is. Today his words pass the Liaison's verdict, a relay, the
Terminologist's or the Architect's respecify, the Vision Keeper's decision,
and then the Developer and the Tester. Each hop is a 9B rewrite. The seat
that heard the words, the Liaison at its verdict, writes the criterion's
new text itself, and the respecify mode on a contest goes.

What it buys. Fewer seams where his words are rewritten. DECISIONS names
"drift that compounds across hops while every hop looks locally
reasonable" as what no invariant can see (observed: `rota/DECISIONS.md`,
"What deterministic checks cannot catch"). Three seams on one night become
one.

What it costs. A seam design, 8 to 10 cycles, a mode split and the
re-record of two modes' cases, with the 9 Liaison cases stale on every
`landing.md` change at about 45 minutes of the card (observed:
`plans/operating-facts.md`, frame 234's line). Rulings crossed: it changes
who talks to whom, so it is a composition change and Roman rules it. It
does not cross "A new role is justified by a trust boundary, not a
capability" (observed: `rota/DECISIONS.md`), because it removes a hop and
adds none. The risk: the Liaison's briefs are the most fragile in the
register. Frame 234 cost 12 cycles there (observed: its close).

### 4.4 A different destination

Not recommended. The destination is right and it already names the
measure: "a merged change they can read", "honest about where small models
stop". What is missing is not a new destination but one ratio under it
that every sprint reads first: merges that carry what the principal
declined, over merges. That ratio is in the night 148 read's last row and
in no frame's ends-when but 243's (observed: `plans/night148-read.md`
section 0). The recommendation in section 6 makes it the sprint's ratio.

## 5. Where the loop belongs

**The method is the build session's own loop, written as a rule.** Chain,
seam, reproduce, place, try, pass, night is what the session has done
since "read what the model sent" (Roman, 2026-09-15), ruled piece by piece:
the interview rule of 2026-10-02, the three-part ends-when of 2026-10-03,
the cycles of 2026-10-11, the placement of 2026-10-12 (observed: the
rulings in `CLAUDE.md` and the stack's rules). The composition puts the
build session as "a second instance with its own rules ... one strong
model, one context, no wiring" (observed: `plans/composition.md`, The
build session). The loop's judgement, which move and whether a count
holds, is the session's and stays there.

**Three of its steps are instruments, and they belong in rota's tools.**
Each is rewritten by an agent per frame today.

1. The chain and the count. Step 1 reads the night's records for the turns
   that carry the wall. Step 4 counts a door "on every night's records:
   fires on a of n instances, b of N right calls". Both are queries over
   the night databases, which frame 144 made a test tier. A night read on
   Opus costs 320k to 339k (observed: the day read, section 5), and
   `python -m rota.tools.nightreport` already prints the night. The count
   should be one tool call, not a section of a 200k brief.
2. The replay. Step 2's control replay and step 4's try run on
   `probes/frame37/interview_turn.py`, which "does not reproduce the
   night's completion" without the runner's `num_predict`, and on
   `f243/interview_conv.py` in a scratchpad (observed:
   `plans/operating-facts.md`, the probe line of 2026-10-14; frame 243's
   status of 01:37). The try's five perturbations and its placebo line are
   a harness nobody has written. Until it is in `rota/tools`, every try is
   a 60k to 100k cycle that re-makes it.
3. The judge. Step 3's last answer, "the principal on one line, a stronger
   model or an executable check", is a capability of rota, not a step of
   the session. The composition says "Capability comes from wiring. No
   brief grants what is not wired" (observed: The parties). Section 4.1 is
   that capability.

**One tension with a ruling.** The design's order of choice puts a door or
a rule first, "because a door shortens the seat's loop to one turn"
(observed: `plans/wall-method-design.md` 0.1). Roman ruled prompts over
guards on 2026-09-02, "no rule bounds an LLM judgement", and the register
showed "every judgement-shaped guard regressed a register case or gained
nothing" (observed: `plans/operating-facts.md`, the rulings on briefs and
doors). The block's step 3 holds the fact line, "a door that states it",
and the ladder review caught one word rule in a door's coat on frame 243.
The order of choice should name the 2026-09-02 line as its gate: a door
first only where the check is a fact under every model (reasoned: the
ruling and the measurement, against 0.1's wording).

## 6. The recommendation, and the one question

**Run the method with a change in what it is for.** Keep the loop as the
way one wall is fixed. Change three things about what it runs on.

1. The sprint's first line is one ratio: merges that carry what the
   principal declined, over merges, read at every night. The method runs
   on the walls a night shows on that ratio, and on a wall that stops a
   batch. Nothing else is held.
2. The sprint's design frame is the judge at the merge gate, section 4.1,
   8 to 10 cycles and a night. The checkpoint of 4.2 is built inside it as
   an opt-in setting, 2 cycles more, for a principal who prefers his minute
   to a seat's judgement.
3. The open frames that a night has not shown on that ratio, about ninety,
   go to the parked list with their counts. A night that shows one of them
   brings it back. The three instruments of section 5 become frames in
   `rota/tools`, priced 3 cycles each, before the next scope read spends
   200k to re-make them.

The alternative is to run the method as ruled on sprint 6's fifteen rows.
At the method's prices that is about 19 to 24 cycles for five rows, one
sprint, with ten more walls pushed by its night (observed: review 2,
section 6; section 1 above). The count of open frames is then about 110
at the next boundary, and the merge still carries what he declined,
because frame 244 and frame 250 are not in the five.

**The one question for Roman, rated [high].** In plain words: tonight the
night merged three changes, and all three carry something you said no to.
The method fixes that one seat at a time, three frames for one effect,
while ten new frames arrive a day. The recommendation is to put one judge
at the merge, a check that reads your exact words against the change and
fails the merge with your sentence named, and to run the wall method only
on what that judge and a stopped batch show, with the rest of the stack
parked. Is the next design frame the judge at the merge gate, with the
wall method narrowed to its walls? Recommended answer: yes. If no, the
method runs as ruled on sprint 6, and the ratio of 4.4 is still read
first at every night.
