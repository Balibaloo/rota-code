## 3. The five changes proposed

Each is Roman's to rule. Recommended: all five.

1. A status line is three lines: the verdict, the numbers, the pointer.
   The report file holds the rest.
2. A night cycle pushes a frame only when it is a held frame's wall.
   Other walls go to one list the night read ranks, and the read pushes
   at most three. The rest become one line each in a walls register.
3. A review runs per pass on code. A prompt or text pass gets its check
   at the night.
4. A design goes to Roman once, after its review, and not per revision.
   Revisions are the session's until then.
5. The take reads a print of the held frames, not the stack. That is
   frame 287's frame 2.
