## The reading

1. **The stall compounds, the errors do not.** The count of new walls
   falls. The cost of each week rises. The fault is in the conversion of
   a placed wall into a built fix.
2. **The builder shows rota's own pathology.** Dry run 8 failed because a
   Developer bent the code to a test nobody judged. The build stored 32
   reds as the gate's baseline, which is the same move.
3. **A session steers at the scale its instruments show.** The state
   print counts frames. `rota/COMPLETION.md` went uncited for 44 days.
   Every change of direction in the window came from Roman, nine of them.
4. **Rules exist and do not fire.** Rule C39 says a resume stops when it
   costs as much as a cold pass. The condition holds since 2026-11-04.
   Rule C76 of 2026-10-26 asks for the root read, and Roman asked for it
   again on 2026-11-08.
5. **The wiring thesis is not settled.** The wiring grows and does not
   converge. No read of dry runs 4 to 8 places a wall as a model's limit,
   and no door was removed. Corrected 2026-11-10 by the review of frame
   363: one older wall is placed so, frame 201's interview. Roman's "less doors and more in depth test cases" of
   2026-10-28 is the one recorded push the other way.
