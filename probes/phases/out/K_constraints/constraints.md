Here are three commitments that this project has made to someone outside of it:

1. **HEADLINE:** Provide a way for users to generate notes based on templates or intents
**WHO NOTICES:** Users who want to create new notes with specific content, and will notice if the plugin is not working as expected.

2. **HEADLINE:** Load global intents from a specific note and allow users to reload them
**WHO NOTICES:** Users who have set up global intents in their settings, and will notice if the plugin is not loading or reloading these intents correctly.

3. **HEADLINE:** Merge global intents with note-specific intents when creating new notes
**WHO NOTICES:** Users who want to select an intent from either their global settings or the active note's contents, and will notice if the merged list of intents is not being displayed correctly.
